# 📊 Executive Sales & Margin Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end business intelligence dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance, profitability trends, and sub-category losses using **Power BI**, **Power Query**, and **DAX**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project simulates an enterprise-level sales review for a global retail dataset (10,000+ transaction records). The goal was to transition raw transactional data into actionable business insights for executive leadership, focusing on high-performing product segments and heavy loss-making areas.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (Data cleaning, type casting, merging)
* **Modeling & DAX:** Custom `_All Measures` table, Calculated Measures (Total Sales, Profit Margin %, Total Orders)
* **Visualization:** Power BI Desktop (Custom corporate Navy & Slate theme, Diverging conditional formatting)

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Metrics & Visuals
1. **Top KPI Scorecards:** 
   - Total Sales: **$2.30M**
   - Total Orders: **5K**
   - Total Profit: **$286.40K**
   - Profit Margin: **12.47%**
2. **Monthly Trends (Line Chart):** Tracks seasonality and Month-over-Month (MoM) growth for both Sales and Profit.
3. **Profitability by Sub-Category (Stacked Bar Chart):** Highlights top-performing assets (Copiers, Phones) vs. critical loss-making segments (Tables at -$18K margin).
4. **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic regional (East, West, Central, South) and multi-year filtering.

---

## 💡 Executive Insights & Business Recommendations
* **Growth Driver:** The *Technology* category drives over **50% of total company profit margins**, indicating a strong priority area for inventory and marketing investments.
* **Loss Mitigations:** The *Furniture* category (specifically the *Tables* sub-category) yields a negative margin (-11%), driven by excessive discounting. 
* **Actionable Strategy:** Implementation of strict **discount caps** on Tables and **vendor price renegotiation** to restore profitability.
